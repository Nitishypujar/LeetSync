## Service and operator

LeetSync is an independent service operated by NITISH Y PUJAR in India. It captures a user's own accepted LeetCode submissions through a browser extension and can write them to a GitHub repository selected by that user. LeetSync is not affiliated with or endorsed by LeetCode or GitHub.

## Accounts and permission

Users must control the connected LeetCode and GitHub accounts and have the rights needed to submit and publish the code they sync. Users are responsible for reviewing repository visibility and permissions. The current setup creates or reuses a public `leetcode-solutions` repository; source code and commit history in a public repository can be copied and viewed by anyone.

Users must not use the service to access another person's account, submit code they are not authorized to publish, interfere with the service, or violate applicable law or the terms of LeetCode, GitHub, browser stores, or other providers. LeetSync relies on LeetCode web pages and endpoints that are not an official public API; availability or compatibility may change. Counsel must determine whether the intended integration and store distribution are permitted.

## Availability and changes

Sync may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, or unavailable because of browser, provider, network, database, or service failures. Historical backfill is not included unless expressly offered. LeetSync may change or discontinue features and may suspend access for abuse or security reasons, subject to applicable law and required notice.

## Disconnecting and deleting

Users may disconnect GitHub or LeetCode and may request deletion through the dashboard. Disconnecting preserves existing service history and repository commits. Deleting the LeetSync account removes active service records; it does not remove commits already written to GitHub, and provider logs/backups may be subject to separate retention. Provider authorization revocation can fail and may require manual removal in the provider's settings.
